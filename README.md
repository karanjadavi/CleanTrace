\## The problem



Air pollution, illegal dumping, and water contamination are persistent, under-monitored threats across Ghana's urban centers. Existing monitoring is sparse, and there's no simple, trustworthy way for citizens to report incidents — or any guarantee that reports, once made, stay unaltered.



\## The solution



CleanTrace combines two data sources into a single, tamper-proof public record, attested on-chain via a Soroban smart contract on Stellar:



\- \*\*Live air quality readings\*\*, pulled automatically from \[OpenAQ](https://openaq.org)'s public sensor network

\- \*\*Citizen-submitted reports\*\* — illegal dumping, smoke, water contamination



Once submitted, a reading can't be silently edited or deleted. Anyone — a journalist, a regulator, a community group — can independently verify the full history for any location.
